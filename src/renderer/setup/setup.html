Step 1

Welcome.

JARVIS is a voice assistant for your Mac. Say “Jarvis, wake up”, then talk. Say “sleep” when you are done.

Everything stays on this Mac unless you pick a cloud service. Keys are encrypted with your macOS Keychain.

    Step 6

    Access

    macOS asks you to allow each thing JARVIS does. Grant them here once, so nothing interrupts you later.

    • MicrophoneTo hear you. Needed.
    • Speech recognitionTo turn what you say into text, on this Mac. Needed.
    • NotificationsFor reminders when the HUD is closed. macOS shows its question with the first one.
    • AccessibilityTo read and use other apps when you ask, like Notes or Finder. Optional.
    • Screen RecordingTo look at a window when a job needs it. Optional.
    • Control iTermTo read and type into your Claude Code sessions. Optional.

    If you said "Don't Allow" before, the button opens the right page in System Settings. Turn JARVIS on there and come back.
